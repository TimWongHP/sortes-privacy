Sortes — Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · applies to the Sortes Android app (com.sortes.app) and the Sortes web app

In short

Sortes has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter — your optional birth details, allergies and diet, your journal, hidden items and number history — is stored only on your device. We, the developer, never receive any of it.

To show dishes, products and card art, the app fetches public data from a few third-party services, described below. Those requests carry only what is needed to answer them.

What stays on your device

DataWhy
Birth date, birth time, country (all optional)To personalise draws (sun sign, season, home cuisine) and to prefer products sold in your country
Allergies and diet (optional)To filter out dishes and packaged foods that might not suit you — filtering happens on your device
Journal of past readings, hidden items, number/letter history, settingsSo you can look back, search, and avoid things you don't use

This data is never uploaded. You can export it to a backup file yourself (the file is yours to keep or share), and you can delete everything from You → Erase all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

What leaves your device, and to whom

ServiceWhat the app sendsWhen
TheMealDBRequests for its public dish list (no personal data)Food readings; cached for a week
Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products FactsThe product type you type (e.g. “shampoo”) and, if you set one, your country as a search filterProduct readings; cached for a day
Wikimedia CommonsRequests for card images and local dish photos (no personal data)When a card with deck art is drawn, or a local dish is shown

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address and basic request information. Their own privacy policies apply to that. Sortes does not send your birth details, allergies, journal or any identifier to them, and nothing is sent to us.

All requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

The lists of food places (from OpenStreetMap) and local dishes ship inside the app, so choosing a place or a local dish sends nothing anywhere. Sortes never asks for your location. If you tap a map link, Google Maps opens with that place or search, and Google's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Sortes is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Randomness and fair play

Readings and number tools are for entertainment and for breaking indecision. They do not predict anything. The lottery tool generates numbers only; it does not sell tickets or take bets.
